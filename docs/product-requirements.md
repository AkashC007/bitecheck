# Product requirements

## Product statement

BiteCheck is a travel-aware restaurant intelligence platform that will combine
restaurant information, transportation accessibility, review themes, and
cross-source confidence signals to generate explainable recommendations through
a conversational interface.

## Initial constraints

- Chicago is the initial geographic scope.
- The initial portfolio version should use free tools and reproducible synthetic
  data.
- Paid services and restricted-source scraping are out of scope.
- Voice remains optional and will use progressive enhancement when introduced.
- Review confidence must never be presented as proof of truthfulness.

## Current milestone boundary

Milestone 11 is complete. The project has synthetic multi-source reviews,
measured theme analysis, explainable confidence and ranking, a responsive
recommendation interface, and explicit multi-turn follow-up state. Optional
browser voice is next; database storage and an AI parser remain future roadmap
work.
